Many ideas and side-projects never see the light of day as a formal scientific publication. Nevertheless, they are functional and potentially useful. With such thoughts in mind, I here catalogue a set of open science computational products that I have created, promoting [FAIR principles](https://openscience.eu/article/infrastructure/guide-fair-principles), scientific collaboration and the acceleration of scientific research.

1. **AnnotatoR: An R package for the creation of general purpose, IonReporter-compatible variant annotation files.** Dionysios Fanidis (2026). [DOI:10.5281/zenodo.21098337](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21098337) [BiobankCy/AnnotatoR](https://github.com/BiobankCy/AnnotatoR)

2. **ReporteR is a Shiny-powered application for the creation of comprehensive genetic diagnostic reports, starting from a small variant spreadsheet with minimum annotation data.** Dionysios Fanidis (2026). [DOI:10.5281/zenodo.21296883](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21296883) [BiobankCy/ReporteR](https://github.com/BiobankCy/ReporteR)
